Keep tracks together, even on shuffle

Links is a Spotify companion app for desktop. Chain tracks like Part 1 and Part 2 together, and they'll always play in order — without turning off shuffle for everything else.

Download for Windows Download for Mac

Requires a Spotify Premium account.

How it works

  1. Install Links and connect your Spotify account.
  2. Search for a track and link it to the ones that should follow it.
  3. Keep listening on shuffle — Links watches for a linked track and queues its partner the moment it starts playing.
